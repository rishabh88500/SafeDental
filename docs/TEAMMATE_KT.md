## 🎯 1. High-Level Project Summary (The "Elevator Pitch")

### What is SafeDental?
SafeDental is a clinical decision-support and AI safety system designed for **emergency dentistry** (acute toothache and odontogenic infections).

### Why does it exist? (The Problem)
1. **The Antibiotic Resistance Crisis**: Dentists prescribe over 10% of all outpatient antibiotics. Official clinical guidelines (**ADA 2019**) strictly mandate **operative treatment** (pulpectomy/root canal therapy or extraction) for localized tooth pain and prohibit antibiotics unless systemic infection signs (fever, spreading swelling) exist. Standard LLMs (ChatGPT, Claude, Llama) fail this test: they hallucinate answers and **prescribe antibiotics in 64.58% of localized toothache cases**, accelerating antibiotic resistance.
2. **Missed Airway Emergencies**: Naive AI models see swollen jaw symptoms and recommend home salt-water rinses or oral pain pills, missing life-threatening emergencies like **Ludwig's Angina** (deep fascial space infection causing airway asphyxiation).
3. **Absence of Abstention**: Standard models answer 100% of queries even when vital clinical facts (fever, swelling, allergies, duration) are missing.

### How does SafeDental solve it? (The Solution)
SafeDental introduces:
1. A **2-Stage Clinical Determinability Gate**: Evaluates whether a case has all required facts before letting an LLM answer.
2. A **Dense Dental Evidence RAG Index**: Grounds approved answers in official **ADA 2019** and **SDCEP 2021** guidelines using FAISS.
3. A **Claim-Level Evidence Verifier**: Verifies that every sentence generated by the model is backed by the retrieved evidence.

### The 3 Study Arms (Crucial for Viva/Presentation):
* **Arm A (Base AI Baseline)**: Unconstrained open-weight model (`meta-llama/llama-3.1-8b-instruct`) without guardrails.
  * *Result*: 64.58% unsafe recommendation rate (prescribes antibiotics empirically, guesses missing facts).
* **Arm B (Gatekeeper Only Ablation)**: 2-stage Determinability Gate without retrieval (evaluates safety prompting alone).
  * *Result*: Halts unsafe recommendations (0.00% unsafe rate), but lacks guideline citations.
* **Arm C (Proposed Full SafeDental System)**: Determinability Gate + FAISS Guideline RAG + Evidence Verifier.
  * *Result*: **0.00% unsafe rate**, **100% safe abstention**, **100% clinical accuracy**, with **verifiable ADA/SDCEP citations**.

## 🔍 3. The Two Core Technical Innovations

### Innovation 1: The 2-Stage Determinability Gate (`src/determinability/`)
* **Stage 1 (Hard Safety Red Flags - `rules.py`)**:
  * Detects airway threats (stridor, dyspnea, floor-of-mouth swelling, Ludwig's Angina).
  * Detects severe trismus (mouth opening $< 15\text{mm}$).
  * Detects drug-seeking prompt injections (fake doctors ordering Oxycodone).
  * **Clinical Negation Engine**: Built with regex lookback windows to prevent false alarms on negative clinical findings (e.g. distinguishing *"No trismus"* from *"Trismus present"*).
  * *Outcome*: Immediate `ESCALATE` (Hospital referral).
* **Stage 2 (5-Point Clinical Safety Checklist - `checklist.py`)**:
  * Evaluates completeness across 5 mandatory categories:
    1. 📍 **Tooth Location**: Verified tooth number, arch, or quadrant.
    2. ⏱️ **Pain Onset & Duration**: Verified acute duration in days/hours.
    3. 🎈 **Swelling Status**: Verified localized, spreading, or absent.
    4. 🌡️ **Fever Status**: Verified temperature or afebrile status.
    5. 💊 **Allergies & Medical History**: Verified drug allergies (penicillin, NSAIDs).
  * *Outcome*: If any fact is missing $\rightarrow$ System pauses and dispatches `ASK` (asking patient clarifying questions instead of guessing).

### Innovation 2: Dental Evidence RAG & Verifier (`src/rag/`)
* **Guidelines Ingested**: ADA 2019, SDCEP 2021, AAE 2020, AAOMS.
* **Embedding Model**: `BAAI/bge-small-en` (384 dimensions, sentence-transformers).
* **Vector Index**: FAISS Flat Inner Product.
* **Retrieval Metric**: **100% Recall@3** and **0.967 MRR**.
* **Evidence Verifier (`evidence_verifier.py`)**: Checks claim support ratios against retrieved chunks.

## 🎯 7. Viva & Faculty Presentation FAQ (Exact Answers)

### Q1: "Why did you choose acute dental pain instead of general medicine?"
> **Answer**: Dentistry is responsible for **10% of all outpatient antibiotic prescriptions worldwide**. Antibiotic misuse in dental emergencies is one of the largest drivers of global antimicrobial resistance. Furthermore, acute dental pain has clear, evidence-based clinical guidelines (ADA 2019) that mandate operative treatment over systemic antibiotics, making it the ideal high-stakes domain to evaluate AI safety and determinability.

### Q2: "What is the difference between Determinability and Determinism?"
> **Answer**: 
> * **Determinism** means the model produces the exact same output given the same input (achieved via `temperature=0.0` and `seed=42`).
> * **Clinical Determinability** is a medical epistemic concept: it means *whether the clinical presentation contains sufficient diagnostic information to justify an active medical decision*. If crucial facts are missing, the case is non-determinable, and an ethical clinician (or AI) must withhold advice and ask clarifying questions.

### Q3: "What prevents data leakage during your evaluation?"
> **Answer**: We implemented a sealed data guard (`src/utils/data_guard.py`). The test split (`data/cases/test.jsonl`) is locked with cryptographic checksums and raises a `SealedTestAccessError` if imported or accessed during development. All model prompts, rule parameters, and threshold tuning were developed strictly on the train and dev splits.

### Q4: "How do you know SafeDental's improvement isn't just random luck?"
> **Answer**: We ran **McNemar's Paired Chi-Squared Test with continuity correction** comparing Arm A and Arm C. The test yielded $\chi^2 = 29.03$ with a $p$-value of **$p < 0.000001$**, well below the $\alpha = 0.05$ threshold, proving that the reduction in unsafe recommendations is statistically significant.

### Q5: "What if an emergency case like Ludwig's Angina arrives?"
> **Answer**: Ludwig's Angina triggers **Stage 1 Hard Red-Flag Rules** in the Determinability Engine (checking for floor-of-mouth elevation, bilateral submandibular swelling, or stridor). The system immediately bypasses LLM advice generation and outputs a hardcoded emergency escalation (`ESCALATE`) instructing immediate hospital emergency room referral.
